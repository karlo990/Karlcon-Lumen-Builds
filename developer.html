3D model generation

Paste your Meshy key once. Each concept's cropped render is sent to Meshy image-to-3D, and the finished model is optimised in this browser (compressed geometry, JPEG textures) and stored in the library permanently. It is generated once and every visitor after that loads the small stored copy — no repeat credits, no expiring links. The engineered KARLCON skylight is fitted on top in the viewer; use Skylight to adjust where it sits.
